Calories & Macros 📊
Per Bun: 560 calories
56g | 33g | 21g F
 
For the hot honey chicken 
- 800g chicken breast
- 1 tbsp paprika
- 1 tbsp onion powder
- 1 tbsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp cayenne pepper
- 2 tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp salt
- Cooking spray (roughly 1 tbsp oil in total)
- 1/2 red onion
- 2 tbsp sweetcorn
- 90g low fat cheese (I used 50% less fat cheddar)
- 2 tsp oregano
- 60ml hot sauce (I used franks red hot)
- Drizzle of honey

Sriracha mayo
- 2 tbsp light mayo
- 1 tbsp sriracha
- 1 tsp honey

Other ingredients
- 6 aldi brioche burger buns
- 100g low fat Swiss cheese (I used leerdamer 50% less fat - sub with low fat cheddar)
- Sliced pickles
- 250g mozzarella (use reduced fat is you have it - divided between 6 burgers)

Garlic butter (optional)
- 1 tbsp light butter
- 1/2 tsp garlic paste
- parsley flakes

Oven bake at 190°C / 375°F | 10-12 minutes 🔥

Storage & Heating ♨️
- if storing, wrap in foil once cooled and store in the fridge, to reheat microwave for 30 seconds to a minute to remove the chill then air fry to get toasty again
