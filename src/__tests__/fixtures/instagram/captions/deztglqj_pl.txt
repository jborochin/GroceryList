Chipotle avocado classic mayo
Chipotle peppers and choosen classic mayo emulsified till smooth 

Lemon garlic sauce
In a pan add lemon, soy sauce, minced garlic, butter, avocado oil, black pepper, and honey. Cook till reduced while mixing stop when thickened 

Cucumber and cabbage salad
In a container add sliced cucumber and cabbage then add avocado oil, fresh lemon, salt, pepper, garlic powder, fresh parsley. Shake well and enjoy
