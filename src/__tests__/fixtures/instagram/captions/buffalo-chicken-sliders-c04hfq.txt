Buffalo chicken sliders 🍔🍗

Recipe (makes 6 sliders) 
Ingredients for the chicken marinade 
600g chicken mini fillets 
2 tbsp Cajun seasoning 
Juice of 1 lemon
Olive oil 
Salt and pepper 
Mix everything in a bowl and bake or fry until fully cooked. 
Once the chicken is slightly cooled down, cut into bite size pieces. 
You will need also 
A Bottle of buffalo sauce (160ml) 
1 tsp garlic powder 
Black pepper and salt 
3 tbsp of Home made or store bought ranch sauce 
Grated cheese (mine was cheddar) 
Cheese slices  6x 
Any dinner rolls. 
Melted butter (20g) 
Chopped fresh parsley 
2 garlic cloves, minced or chopped 
Pinch of sweet paprika and black pepper 

Add the chicken cooked pieces to a bowl,add the buffalo sauce, garlic powder and black pepper and mix well. Slice the rolls in half lengthwise or use pre sliced rolls. 
In a baking dish arrange the bottom half of the dinner rolls, arrange the cheese slices , add all the chicken, range sauce and grated cheese, place the other half of the white rolls on top , in a small bowl add the melted butter, chopped garlic, chopped parsley, pinch of sweet paprika and black pepper and mix well, brush  the tops of the rolls with the garlic butter.. Bake on 180C until cheese is melted and the bread is golden brown. 

Enjoy !!!
