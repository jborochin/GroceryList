Macros per serving (1 cup rice per serving) (X3): 590 Calories |55g P | 47g C | 17g F

For the chicken:
1.5lbs chicken breast, cubed
Salt & Pepper, to taste
1 teaspoon Garlic powder
1 tsp chili powder
1 tsp paprika 
1/4 tsp cumin
Olive oil, 1 tbsp to marinate
Avocado oil spray, to cook

Street Corn Salsa:
2 cups corn 🌽 
1/4 cup light mayonnaise 
1/4 cup cotija cheese 🧀 
1 lime juice and zest 🍋‍🟩 
1 Jalapeño, finely diced 🌶️ 
2-3 tbsp chopped cilantro 🌿 
1 avocado, cubed 🥑 
1 tsp Tajin, (optional)

Enjoy 😍
