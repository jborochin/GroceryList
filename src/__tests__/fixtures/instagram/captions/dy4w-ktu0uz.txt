Creamy Sun-Dried Tomato Pasta 🍝

This one went viral for a reason and I had to make it myself. 20 minutes and it tastes like you cooked all day.

Comment PASTA and I’ll send you what you see ⬇️

16 oz spaghetti | 4 tbsp butter | 4 garlic cloves minced | 1 cup sun-dried tomatoes oil-packed and drained | 2 cups chicken broth | 8 oz cream cheese softened and cubed | 1½ cups milk | 1 tsp dried basil | ½ tsp black pepper | 1 cup freshly grated parmesan | Red pepper flakes optional
