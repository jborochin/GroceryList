Cinnamon rolls… made in 20 minutes, and every bite is the center of the cinnamon Roll

In depth recipe with pictures on substack!!!

🛒 Ingredients

Dough

1 cup (130g) all purpose flour
1 tsp baking powder
1/4 tsp salt
1 tsp cinnamon
1/3 cup (80ml) milk (or half milk, half heavy cream)
1 tsp vanilla
1 tbsp melted butter

Coating

1 tbsp melted butter
3 tbsp sugar
1-2 Tsp cinnamon

Vanilla Bean Glaze ✨

4 tbsp powdered sugar
2 tsp heavy cream
1/2 tsp vanilla
Vanilla bean or vanilla bean paste
👨‍🍳 Instructions

1️⃣ Preheat oven to 375°F and grease or line your pan

2️⃣ In a bowl, whisk together flour, baking powder, salt, and cinnamon

3️⃣ Warm milk slightly, then mix in vanilla and melted butter

4️⃣ Pour wet into dry and mix until a soft dough forms, stop as soon as it comes together, don’t overmix

5️⃣ Lightly flour your surface, roll dough into a log, flatten slightly, and cut into 20 even bites

6️⃣ Toss each piece in melted butter, then coat fully in cinnamon sugar

7️⃣ Place in pan in a single layer with a little space between each, no stacking

8️⃣ Bake 9 to 10 minutes, until lightly golden, don’t overbake

9️⃣ While baking, mix glaze until smooth, adjust thickness if needed

🔟 Let bites cool 1 to 2 minutes, drizzle glaze over the top while warm, and enjoy

⏱ Time & Servings
Prep 10 min
Cook 10 min
Serves 2 to 3
🔥 Pro Tip

Mix just until the dough comes together, overmixing is what makes these dense instead of soft and pillowy.
