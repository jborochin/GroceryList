ULTIMATE BREAKFAST SANDWICH
767 Cal | 70g Protein | $4.00

Ingredients

Plain Bagels  8x |  760g
Top Sirloin  34 oz |  964g
Eggs  8x |  400g
Low Fat Cheddar  1 ½ cups |  168g
Beef Bacon  8 strips |  224g
Honey  4 tbsp |  84g
Salt, pepper, garlic powder

Directions

1. Season the top sirloin with salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
2. Cook the steak in a pan on medium heat for a few minutes per side until it reaches 125°F (52°C) internal temperature, then let it rest for 5 minutes.
3. Dice the rested steak into bite-sized pieces.
4. Cook the beef bacon in a pan until crisp, then remove and set aside.
5. In the same pan, crack in the eggs and scramble or fry as preferred.
6. Top the eggs with the low fat cheddar cheese and allow it to melt.
7. Toast the sliced bagels in the pan drippings until golden brown.
8. Assemble the sandwiches by layering the steak, cheesy eggs, and bacon on the toasted bagel, finishing with a drizzle of honey.

Reheating

Fridge: Wrap in foil and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 10-12 minutes.
Frozen: Thaw overnight in the fridge, then follow fridge instructions.
