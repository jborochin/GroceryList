CREAMY CHICKEN AND BACON GNOCCHI 

Garlicky chicken, crispy bacon and soft gnocchi all cooked in one pan 😮‍💨

The gnocchi soaks up all the gorgeous flavours as it cooks directly in the sauce!

Serves 4 
Cals 698 | Pro 45 | Carb 80 | Fat 21

Ingredients ~ 
6 rashes of bacon
1 onion
400g chicken breast
1 tsp each of garlic/paprika/Italian herbs 
500g gnocchi 
300ml hot ham stock
200ml single cream
75g red leister cheese 
Chives 

Method ~ 
*Cook bacon in a cold pan till crispy, remove and leave the bacon fat behind
*Add chicken and onion with the seasonings and cook for 10 mins 
*Add the gnocchi, stir to combine, pour in the cream and stock, cover with a lid and simmer for 3-5 mins
*Stir in the cheese, top with bacon and chives

Hope you enjoy!
