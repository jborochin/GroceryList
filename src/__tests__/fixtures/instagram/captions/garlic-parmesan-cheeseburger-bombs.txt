Garlic parmesan cheeseburger bombs 🍔

RECIPE ⬇️
* 1 lb 80/20 ground beef
* 1 tsp garlic powder
* 1 tsp onion powder 
* 2 tsp Worcestershire 
* Minced garlic 
* Salt & pepper to taste 
* Optional add-ins: caramelized onions, bacon
* Mild cheddar, large cubes
* Shredded mozzarella 
* 1 can biscuit dough (Pillsbury original flakey layers)
* 1 egg, whisked (for egg wash)
* Parmesan 

Garlic butter:
* 3 tbs salted butter, melted
* 2 cloves garlic, finely minced
* Dried or fresh parsley

1. Cook the ground beef on medium heat. Once browned, add minced garlic and all the seasonings. Stir for a minute. Set aside. 
2. Preheat oven to 375F
3. Take each biscuit and stretch it into a 5 inch circle. Add 2 spoonfuls of seasoned beef, some mozzarella, and a cube of cheese in the middle. 
4. Fold the dough around the filling and pinch the edges tightly to seal. Pull it into a tight ball. Rest 5 min.
5. Place them seam side down on a parchment-lined baking sheet. Brush a thin layer of egg wash and sprinkle fresh grated parmesan.
6. Bake at 375F for 15-18 min until deeply golden.
7. Brush on garlic butter and sprinkle more parmesan. Serve immediately.
